Directory

Who to ask, and where the map continues.

Local organisers and studios first, then the international resources that carry the form's listings, archives and festivals.

Short answer

Contact Improvisation has no central directory because it has no central body. Local listings are held by the organisers themselves. The closest thing to a global index is the CI World Jam Map at contactimprov.com, which lists sessions by country and state, including a Florida page covering Miami, Sarasota, Gainesville and Jacksonville.

Teachers and organisers in Miami

Recurring teachers we can confirm today

Contact Improvisation has no certification body, so this list is built from what teachers and organisers publish about themselves rather than from a register. We have not yet confirmed a Miami-based CI teacher's own public listing. If you teach or organise here, send us your page.

Studios and organisations

International resources

These are the sites that actually hold the form's listings, archives and gatherings. Each one was opened and checked when it was added here.

  • Contact Improvisation World Resource

    The international hub for the form, run since the 1990s: a world jam map, an event calendar, a teacher directory, member listings and a links index.

  • CI World Jam Map: Florida

    The community's own Florida listing. It is the closest thing to a South Florida jam directory that exists, and it is maintained by whoever sends corrections.

  • Contact Quarterly

    The journal that grew out of the 1975 newsletter, and the form's main written archive for four and a half decades.

  • Earthdance

    A long-running movement and improvisation centre in Plainfield, Massachusetts, hosting workshops, residencies and jams.

  • Touch&Play Global

    Retreats, workshops and relational learning built around contact and consent practice.

  • Contact Improvisation Dance Canada

    A national CI resource and listing site, useful as a model for how a country's jams get organised and published.

  • Contact improvisation (encyclopaedia entry)

    A starting point for the form's definitions, lineage and influences, with references.

Questions about the directory

Who teaches contact improvisation in Miami?

Contact improvisation has no certification body, so there is no register to consult. The practical route is to ask the studios and organisations that work with partner and improvisational movement in Miami-Dade, several of which are listed on this page, and to ask at any jam you attend who is teaching locally.

How do I get listed here?

Send the name, the city, what you teach or host, and a link to your own page. There is no fee and no membership. Listings are checked against your own published information before they go up.

Do you list jams outside Miami?

The focus is Miami-Dade and Broward. Sessions elsewhere in Florida are linked through the CI World Jam Map, which covers the whole state and the world.

Add yourself

No fee, no membership, no committee. If you teach, host or organise in South Florida, this page exists to point at you.
